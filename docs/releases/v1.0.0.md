Veilbound's first release brings its offline sword fighting to GitHub. Choose Kael or Nyra and fight through the Three Seals, challenge a friend in Local Versus, or see how far you can go in Survival.

- Arcade, Quick Duel, Local Versus, Survival, and Training
- Two fighters with distinct movement and attack timing
- Pixel art arenas, including Eclipse Citadel, with original music and sound effects
- Combos, parries, dodges, projectiles, and Veil Breaks
- Keyboard and gamepad support, remappable primary controls, and saved records
- A browser Move Lab for comparing attack timing and combo damage

To play, download the source ZIP below, import `project.godot` in Godot 4.6, and press F5.
