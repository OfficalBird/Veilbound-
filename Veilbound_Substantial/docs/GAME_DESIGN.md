# Veilbound Game Design Notes

## Core fantasy

Veilbound is intended to become a fast 2D martial-fantasy fighter where positioning, timing, defense, and resource management matter as much as raw combo execution.

## Combat pillars

### Readable offense
Every move should have a visible commitment: windup, active time, and recovery. Strong attacks do more damage and guard pressure but are easier to punish.

### Active defense
Blocking is safe but costs stamina under pressure. A fresh block briefly opens a parry window, rewarding timing instead of indefinite turtling.

### Mobility with a cost
Dodge grants short invulnerability but consumes stamina, preventing infinite escape loops.

### Resource tension
Stamina is shared by heavy attacks, dodge, projectiles, and defense. Spending aggressively can leave the player unable to escape or block safely.

## Current move set

- Light 1: fast jab, low damage
- Light 2: follow-up strike, moderate hitstun
- Light 3: combo finisher with strong knockback
- Heavy: slower, high damage, high guard damage
- Block: reduces damage and knockback
- Parry: short timing window at block startup
- Dodge: fast horizontal invulnerability movement
- Veil Burst: ranged stamina-based projectile

## Future character identity

The current fighter is a systems prototype. Final characters should have distinct silhouettes, weapon styles, frame data, movement values, and signature mechanics rather than palette swaps.
